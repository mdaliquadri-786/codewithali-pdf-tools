Built Different: What Makes CodeWithAli Special

Most online PDF converters fail when handling non-English alphabets, complex layouts, or confidential documents. Here is why users switch to CodeWithAli.

Universal Language & RTL Engine

Full native support for Arabic (العربية), Urdu (اردو), Telugu (తెలుగు), Hindi, Persian, and 100+ languages. Right-to-Left script flow and font ligatures remain intact without scrambled characters.

100% In-Browser Privacy

Your sensitive bank statements, contracts, invoices, and IDs are processed right inside your browser using client-side WebAssembly and PDF.js. No files uploaded to foreign servers.

True Document Fidelity

Near-identical results to your original document. Headings, margins, image placements, and paragraph structures are strictly preserved in Word, text, and PDF exports.

No Limits & 100% Free Forever

No 15MB file size traps, no "2 free conversions per hour" limits, and no paid subscription paywalls. Unlimited document manipulation built for everyone.
